# Play Store listing (copy into Play Console)

**App name** (30 max): Iqamah Times

**Short description** (80 max):
Masjid iqamah times, prayer reminders and adhan. Pick your masjid and go.

**Full description** (4000 max):
Never miss the congregation prayer. Iqamah Times shows today's iqamah times for your masjid, counts down to the next prayer and reminds you before it is time to leave.

* Choose your masjid from a searchable list
* Today's iqamah times for Fajr, Zuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha and Jumu'ah, plus the next 7 days
* Live countdown to the next iqamah, with the Hijri and regular date
* Reminders before iqamah: pick 10, 20, 30 or any number of minutes you like
* Set each prayer to Off, Reminder, or Reminder + Adhan
* Reminders arrive on time even when the app is closed
* Works offline once loaded
* Light and dark themes
* No account, no ads, no tracking

Masjid schedules are kept up to date from the masjid's own posted times. Daylight Saving changes and sunset-based iqamah times (for example "5 minutes after Maghrib adhan") are handled automatically.
