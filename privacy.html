Privacy Policy

Iqamah Times · Last updated: October 2026

Iqamah Times shows iqamah (congregation) prayer times for the masjid you choose and can remind you before each prayer. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics. We do not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal information. The app does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

What stays on your phone

Your choices are saved only on your own device: the masjid you selected, your reminder settings (how many minutes before iqamah, and which prayers) and your light or dark preference. They never leave your phone. Removing the app deletes them.

Internet use

The app downloads masjid names and prayer schedules (plain data files) from a website hosted on GitHub Pages so times stay up to date. This request contains no personal information. As with any website, GitHub, the host, may see your device's IP address in its server logs under its own privacy statement.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show your prayer reminders.
  • Exact alarms: so a reminder arrives at the minute you chose, even when the app is closed.
  • Internet: to download the masjid schedules described above.

Reminders are created on your device. No notification service or outside server is involved.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
